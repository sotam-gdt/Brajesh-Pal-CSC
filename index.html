About Our Center

We are an authorized Common Service Centre (CSC) operating under the Digital India initiative of the Government of India. Our center is run by Brajesh Pal and has been serving citizens of Madhya Pradesh with reliable, fast, and affordable digital services.

Our goal is to bring government services closer to every citizen — especially those in rural and semi-urban areas — eliminating the need to travel long distances to government offices.
